De-risking offshore08 / 14
Your three fears, answered

Quality, revision risk, communication — each with a mechanism.

QualityEvery model is QA-gated against the BEP and EIR — model-health checks, Navisworks clash validation and a documented review at the S0 → S1/S2 transition. No model is issued to your CDE until it passes the QA gate against the agreed LOD.
Revision riskISO 19650 CDE workflow with controlled status codes (S0→S4→A/B): every issue traceable, single-source-of-truth, full revision history per container. No overwriting, no lost markups.
CommunicationA UK-based representative — same time zone, local meetings, no language gap. Work flows to the BEP and MIDP/TIDP dates, with issue-tracked clash reports.
