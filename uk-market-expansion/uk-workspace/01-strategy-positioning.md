# ECY UK — Strategy & Positioning

## The one-line position
**We are the offshore BIM production engine behind UK practices, contractors and surveyors — not a competitor to UK design.** The client relationship and design intent stay with them; we take the modelling load, on their standards.

## Why this position (not "UK architecture firm")
Competing on UK architectural design from Türkiye is slow, low-trust and crowded. Competing as a *production partner* is fast, repeatable and in demand: UK teams face rising ISO 19650 duties, a hard hiring market and deadline spikes they can't permanently staff for. We sell capacity and compliance, not design taste.

## Three differentiators (lead with these — first 3 slides)
1. **A UK-based contact** — Rami Daşkın, 26 years in the UK. Dissolves the offshore objection (time zone, language, accountability).
2. **Autodesk Expert Elite founder** — held by only ~250 professionals worldwide. Verified, scarce credibility.
3. **Proven delivery** — 218 projects, 17 countries, ISO 19650 throughout; real Scan-to-BIM and coordination evidence.

## Target segments
- **Architecture practices** — overflow Revit production when deadlines stack up.
- **Contractors / subcontractors** — federated coordination & clash detection; de-risk delivery.
- **Survey firms** — Scan-to-BIM: they capture the cloud, we deliver the as-built model.
- **MEP / BIM consultancies** — white-label delivery partner, under their brand and BEP.

## Why now (UK market, 2025–2026)
- Hardest hiring market in years — construction & engineering the worst-hit sector (BCC, 2024); ~251.5k extra workers needed by 2028 (CITB).
- BIM headcount is a heavy fixed cost — Coordinator c.£34k–£54k, Manager up to c.£80k, before on-costs.
- Pipeline recovering as build costs climb (RICS) — more delivery, less room to staff up.
- Standards non-negotiable — ISO 19650 on public work; Building Safety Act "golden thread" on higher-risk buildings.
