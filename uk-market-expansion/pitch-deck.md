## Slide 4 — Why now (UK pazar koşulları)
**The modelling load is rising. The people aren't.**
- Hardest hiring market in years — construction & engineering are the UK's worst-hit sector for recruitment difficulty (BCC, 2024).
- BIM headcount is a heavy fixed cost — a Coordinator runs c.£34k–£54k, a Manager up to c.£80k, before on-costs.
- Pipeline is recovering as build costs climb — more delivery, less room to staff up permanently (RICS).
- Standards are non-negotiable — ISO 19650 on UK public work; Building Safety Act 'golden thread' on higher-risk buildings.

*Rakam şeridi:* 82% (constr. & eng. hiring difficulty · BCC Q2 2024) · +17% (forward workload · RICS) · ~251.5k (extra workers by 2028 · CITB)

## Slide 7 — UK Standards
**Built to UK standard, not retrofitted to it.**
- **Framework** — ISO 19650-1 to -5 (delivery, operational, exchange & security-minded)
- **Naming** — BS EN ISO 19650-2 UK convention (Project-Originator-Volume-Level-Type-Role-Number); supersedes BS 1192
- **CDE workflow** — S0 WIP → S1–S4 Shared → A/B Published; full revision traceability
- **Stage-aligned** — RIBA Plan of Work 2020 stages 0–7; proven at Stages 5–7

*Naming example:* `WBP-EDO-00-XX-SP-Z-0001`

**(Notes)** Bu slayt uyumdan öte AKICILIK kanıtlar — örnek isimlendirme + kod seti.

## Slide 8 — De-risking offshore
**Quality, revision risk, communication — each with a mechanism.**
- **Quality** — QA-gated against BEP/EIR; model-health, Navisworks clash validation, documented S0→S1/S2 review. No model issued to your CDE until it passes the QA gate against the agreed LOD.
- **Revision risk** — ISO 19650 CDE status codes (S0→S4→A/B): traceable, single-source-of-truth, full revision history. No overwriting, no lost markups.
- **Communication** — UK-based representative; same time zone, local meetings, no language gap. Work flows to BEP and MIDP/TIDP dates with issue-tracked clash reports.

**(Notes)** Korkuları yüksek sesle adlandır, her birine kontrol (söz değil) ile cevap.
