## Who we are
ECY is a BIM delivery studio that helps UK architecture practices, contractors,
survey firms and engineering consultancies scale their BIM output without growing
headcount. Our team brings **15 years of experience across 17 countries and 218
projects**, working to **ISO 19650** information‑management standards.

We are not a competitor to UK practices — we are the production engine behind them.
You keep the client relationship and the design intent; we take the modelling load.

## What we do
- **BIM Modelling (Revit)** — architectural, structural & MEP models at **LOD 300–400**
- **Scan‑to‑BIM** — point cloud to fully parametric, as‑built Revit models
- **Coordination & Clash Detection** — Navisworks federated models, clash reports, issue tracking
- **4D / 5D** — programme (Primavera P6) and cost integration
- **BIM Execution Plans (BEP)** & CDE setup aligned to ISO 19650
- **Architectural Design & Turnkey** (for projects outside the UK)
